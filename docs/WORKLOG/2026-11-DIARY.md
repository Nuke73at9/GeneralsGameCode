### Tiberium Project: music that keeps time, and plays its tracks out
- The user found the music tied to the frame rate and game speed: a track would play half a minute and stop, and sometimes the music stopped for good.
- Causes:
  - The C&C4 music director (`TiberiumMusic`) timed its states in logic frames. Those run at the game speed (60 a second at the skirmish screen's speed 60), so every wait was halved.

  - The music director keeps its own clock in real time, which stands still while the game is paused.
  - Fights, stingers and a fight's end still cut in at once. A change between quiet states now waits for the playing track to end, and the next track is the new state's.
  - A state without tracks of its own lets the track play on; when it ends, the exploring tracks take over.
  - MiniAudio times fades and delays by the clock, not by frames.

### Tiberium Project: C&C4's zones in a skirmish
- The user asked for base building as Tiberian Twilight has it: zones, shown in each holder's colour, and a skirmish option for how far a base may grow:
  - Pre-zones: structures only in the player's start zone; Crawlers still deploy and make units anywhere.
  - Expansion zones: the start zone, and more zones taken by capturing their node (C&C4's TCN), building an outpost in one that is nobody's, or deploying a Crawler there.
  - Free zones: structures anywhere, as Zero Hour; zones are still taken as above.
  - In every mode a Crawler arrives in one of its player's zones, GDI's dropped from the sky and Nod's drilling up from underground.
  - The user's answers: zones are made from the map, land only; allies' starts near each other share one bigger zone; all three ways of taking a zone; the arrival by faction.
- Skirmish Match Options: "Tiberium Zones: Off / Pre-zones / Expansion zones / Free zones" (bits 16 and 17 of the match rules, `AbramGameRules::getZoneMode`; they travel with the game options and replays). Off is the default, so Zero Hour plays as before.
- New Zero Hour logic system `TiberiumZones`, made when the game starts (new and loaded games alike, from the map):
  - A 20-unit grid over the playable map. Land is the pathfinder's clear ground, rubble or structures, not under water.
  - Seeds: each start (`Player_N_Start` of the occupied slots); allies' starts within `ZoneLinkDistance` are one zone, bridged by seeds along the line between them. Then the supplies and tech buildings away from the starts (those within 320 of each other make one zone), then the land left, wherever there is room for a zone.
  - Each land cell goes to the seed nearest it over land (a fill with integer costs and a fixed order, the same on every machine), as far as `ZoneHomeRadius` (700) or `ZoneExpansionRadius` (420) reach. Zones too small for a base are dropped.
  - Who holds an expansion zone is worked out every second from what stands in it: the player of its oldest claim (a captured node, or a finished command centre, factory, outpost or deployed Crawler) holds it. Nothing needs saving.
  - `TiberiumGameData` fields: `ZoneHomeRadius`, `ZoneExpansionRadius`, `ZoneLinkDistance`, `ZoneNode`.
- Building: `BuildAssistant::isLocationLegalToBuild` refuses a structure outside its player's zones with the new code `LBC_OUTSIDE_ZONE` (with pre-zones and expansion zones). An outpost (anything that claims) may go in an expansion zone that is nobody's. The player is told why ("Structures can only be built in your start zone." / "...in your zones. Take more with a zone's node, an outpost or a Crawler."). The skirmish AI goes through the same check.
- New module `TiberiumZoneNodeUpdate`: C&C4's node. While one side's units (a player and its allies) stand within `CaptureRadius` and no enemy does, it turns to that player in `CaptureTime` (C&C4's 20 s); an enemy's node is first brought back to nobody's in `DecaptureTime` (30 s). Contested, it waits; left alone, the progress fades. It shows GDI's light (USER_1) or Nod's (USER_2). With expansion and free zones, one is placed in each expansion zone of a new game (the `ZoneNode` object, `C4_GoalStructure`).
- New module `TiberiumArrivalUpdate`: a unit that a factory makes, in a game with zones, arrives in its player's zone nearest the factory's rally point (at the rally point when it is in one). The model is drawn falling from `DropHeight` or rising from `DrillDepth` (the instance matrix: drawing only), the unit held meanwhile. Crawlers packed up from their structure and units on the map from the start do not.
- New client module `TiberiumZoneView`:
  - On the radar, each zone's border in its holder's colour (grey: nobody's), and each node as a dot.
  - Over the world, when the HUD's new Zones button is lit or while a structure is being placed with pre-zones or expansion zones: the borders on the ground (two zones meeting: a line in each one's colour), each zone's holder at its middle ("Your start zone", "<player> - zone", "Unclaimed zone"), and a node's capture bar while it is being taken.
